Features

Polycarbonate material

This product is made of polycarbonate (PC), which is not prone to cracking and can protect your belongings from the risk of impact and fracture. In addition, it will not deform even at extreme temperatures ranging from -40°C to 120°C, demonstrating excellent heat resistance.

Aluminum-Magnesium Barrier

The corner radius and closure are both made of 6-series aluminum-magnesium alloy. Through reinforced design, the risk of damage is minimized and the luggage is safely protected during the journey.

Collision prevention design

Thickened ergonomic alloy guard ring provides effective protection, buffering and safeguarding for the box body.
